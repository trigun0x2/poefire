        Path of Exile account for loot tracking

        POESESSID cookie
        How do I get it?
        1. Log in on pathofexile.com in your browser.
        2. Open developer tools (F12) → Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → https://www.pathofexile.com.
        3. Copy the value of POESESSID (32 characters) and paste it above.
        It's your whole logged-in session: never share it or show it on stream. poefire stores it only on this PC, readable by your Windows user, and never displays it again. Logging out of the website ends it; then paste a new one.
        Loot tracking
        Stash tabs to watch
        Pick every tab your loot goes into, including the ones Ctrl+click sends items to (currency, fragments…). Items moved to an unwatched tab count as lost. Each tab costs one request per snapshot.

        In-game overlay
        The overlay shows while Path of Exile is the active window, and clicks pass through it. Set the game to Windowed or Windowed Fullscreen: nothing can be drawn over exclusive fullscreen. It only displays information; it never sends input to the game. Windows app only.
        The overlay isn't running: it needs the Windows app's own window (not the browser).

        poefire
        Updates are downloaded from GitHub, checked against their published SHA-256, and installed when poefire restarts. Your settings and history live in %APPDATA%\poefire and are kept.
        Diagnostics

        Something not working (the campaign tracker, run tracking, the overlay…)? Save a debug report and send the .zip to the developer.

        The report has poefire's log, your settings (never your POESESSID), your campaign runs, and the game's system lines from the end of Client.txt (area changes, zone entries, level-ups, deaths). No chat or whispers.

          Where's the game?

          poefire reads the game's log, Client.txt, to time your maps and campaign runs. It finds it by itself for the usual installs, and switches to the right one once the game is running.

          Connect your account (for loot)

          To see what your maps drop, poefire reads your stash and inventory through pathofexile.com, with your session cookie. It stays on this PC and is never shown or logged. Skip this to only time maps and campaign runs.

          1. Log in on pathofexile.com in your browser.
          2. Press F12 → Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → pathofexile.com.
          3. Copy the value of POESESSID and paste it below.
